The `rg`-compatible engine now runs on a parallel fused walk+read+match pipeline (`src/gist/faces/cli/search/engine/parallel.zig`): work-stealing directory walk with immutable per-dir ignore chains + a compiled literal/extension ignore tier, bulk-stat listings, inline index/freshness read-elision loaded asynchronously, a required-literal SIMD line gate (now also under `-w`), and per-worker sorted fragments k-way-merged into byte-identical (sorted) output. Ineligible flag combinations fall through to the proven serial engine unchanged. Warm-tree result: gist beats ripgrep on every benchmarked shape — 1.2x on `--files`, 1.5–1.8x on scoped/filtered searches, 2.9–4x on whole-repo literal queries.
