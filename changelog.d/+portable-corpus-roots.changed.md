A `.` root normalizes to bare relative paths (`joinRoot`), so foreign-tree output is byte-identical to a live scan. Legacy pre-roots artifacts (missing `roots.list`) fall back to `.` on load — a sound superset (elision keys on the persisted path set); atlas v1 reads as corrupt and rebuilds. Verified end-to-end on the CPython corpus: `gist index` inside the foreign tree, indexed-vs-`--no-index` output parity, and ~4× warm elision (11 ms vs 48 ms).
