`walkFresh` now runs its first shard inline on the calling thread and only spawns workers for the rest, so a single-shard walk (a small tree — the common resident-reconcile case, hit on every non-clean query) spawns zero threads instead of paying a spawn+join per query, while a multi-shard walk still saturates every core with the caller taking a share rather than idling on join. Output is byte-identical; this is a scheduling change only.
