A persisted **kinship atlas** (`src/index/atlas/`) gives `relate` its own warm tier: `relate index` snapshots every corpus file's LZJD sketch (plus path table, wall-clock anchor, FNV-1a checksum) atomically to `.local/gist-verify/kinship.atlas`, and `relate status` reports readiness, freshness, and staleness for both the atlas and the optional codex shelf (`relate index --shelf` now builds the quote shelf without shelling out to gist). The sketch verbs (`similar`/`dups`/`clusters`) load the atlas and fold in only files changed since the anchor — re-sketched from live bytes, deletions gated out — so warm answers are byte-identical to a cold rebuild, just ~12× faster (95 ms vs 1.2 s for `similar` over the 22.8k-file live corpus); `--no-index` or a missing atlas falls back to the live build with identical output.
