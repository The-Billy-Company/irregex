The parallel ripgrep engine (pipeline.zig) had regressed two rg-parity fixes present in the serial engine: -g/--iglob whitelist overrides for ignore rules were not respected, and directory-walk errors (e.g. EACCES) were silently swallowed instead of exiting 2 like ripgrep. Both are now ported into the parallel engine, and the line_parity, freshness_fs, and rgsuite test harnesses now exercise both engines (serial forced via the internal GIST_NO_PARALLEL env var), confirming genuine zero-FAIL parity on both.
