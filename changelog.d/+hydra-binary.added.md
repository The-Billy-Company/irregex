The irregex verbs ship as their own product face: a `hydra` binary (`similar` / `dups` / `patterns` + `--schema`) built from `src/faces/hydra/main.zig` over the same kernel, corpus policy, and persisted trigram index as `gist` — one engine, two faces. `make install-gist` installs both (`~/.local/bin/{gist,hydra}`); the Python bindings drive the verbs through `hydra` (`HYDRA_BIN` override); the `gist` CLI sheds them with a redirect stub (exit 2) rather than a silent literal search, and its `--schema` marks the three verbs moved.
