gist serve now poll-multiplexes its accept loop (listener + every connected client in one poll set, one frame per readable client per wakeup), so an idle persistent Session no longer starves other clients in the listen backlog — previously one agent's long-lived warm session blocked every other agent's connect for minutes. The Python binding's Session also arms a 2 s socket deadline on connect/handshake/query (the twin of the Zig client's client_io_timeout_ms), failing open to the certified cold path instead of blocking indefinitely on a busy or wedged daemon.
