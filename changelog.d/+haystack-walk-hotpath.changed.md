Unified the four duplicated corpus walkers (index build, --live, T3 freshness stat-walk, no-prefilter live scan) onto one shared Haystack/Walker abstraction (src/corpus/tree/haystack.zig), and hand-tuned its two per-call hot paths in the process: isSkipDir moved from a 35-entry linear std.mem.eql scan to a comptime std.StaticStringMap (18.5ns to 2.8ns/call, 6.6x, measured over 1786 real repo directory basenames), and the per-file root/rel path join moved from std.fmt.allocPrint to a manual sized alloc + memcpy (20.9ns to 9.9ns/call, 2.1x, measured over 200k calls) since the walk yields 18.9k files but only thousands of directories.
