# Pincer — Anchor Selection for the Two-Probe Literal Filter

A literal scan's throughput is decided by *which* two needle offsets the vector unit compares, not by the instruction sequence that compares them. The kernel's 64-byte two-probe block filter runs at 30–50 GB/s when its anchors are chosen well and at 2.4 GB/s when they are not, a 20× spread with the instruction sequence held fixed.

The standard way to choose those offsets, shared by the Rust `memchr` crate's rare-byte heuristic and by `rarity.zig` here, ranks bytes by individual frequency. That prices a two-position conjunction as `P(a)·P(b)`, which assumes the probes are independent. Text is the worst case for that assumption: the correlated unit is the word, so correlation is strongest at exactly the short distances a needle offers, and a unigram-minimizing selector drifts toward correlated pairs until the conjunction degenerates toward a single-byte filter.

## The Defect and Its Repair

Here that drift was a collapse, because `rarity.zig` clamps its density at 255 and 20 of 26 lowercase letters tie there. With every byte tied, the old strict `<` tie-break never displaced its initializers and returned `o1=0, o2=1`, the adjacent, maximally correlated pair, for 122 of 177 code needles and 78 of 90 prose needles. The shipped selector was consequently worse than the fixed first+last it replaced, on every summary statistic, in both regimes.

Both halves of the conjunction are repaired now. Selection lives in one module, [`src/kernel/scan/anchor.zig`](../../src/kernel/scan/anchor.zig), whose tie-break resolves toward the widest separation instead of the adjacent pair, and `rarity.zig` carries its full range as an unclamped `[256]u16`, regenerated by a checked-in census that a test asserts has no rank inversions and no lowercase letter pinned at the ceiling.

The defect signature that started this — a degenerate needle running 41% slower than a length-matched control finding 19× more matches — now measures 1% faster, the correct direction (§10.1 of [`PROOF.md`](PROOF.md)).

The two repairs are redundant rather than additive, and §10.2 prices all four corners to show it: the table was the larger defect, and the separation tie-break is what keeps the failure graceful if a future census ever re-introduces ties. Read §10.3 before trusting any single trap/control ratio; a cold page cache moves it far enough to imitate the defect on its own.

## The Calibrating Selector

The design the measurements point to beyond the two repairs is not a better table. It is stratified self-calibration: price the candidate pairs on a sample spread in small windows across the buffer being searched, then scan with the winner. §6 of `PROOF.md` measured two natural compactions of the full 4 MB joint table and found both lose to a 512-byte unigram table, which is what ruled the static approach out and pointed at calibration instead.

That design is built, tested, and now shipped in [`src/kernel/scan/calibrate.zig`](../../src/kernel/scan/calibrate.zig). At its shipped 256-byte window and 64 KB budget it reaches 1.03×/1.02×/1.04× of the best possible pair across code, prose, and a heterogeneous buffer (§7.2.a of `PROOF.md`), against the unclamped static table's 1.49×/1.76× (§3, §10.2), for about 0.2% of a scan at that budget (§7.1).

§7.2 of `PROOF.md` said the missing piece was a per-scan plan rather than a bigger gate, and that is what was built. `simd.Plan` is the decision as a value, `simd.planOn` is the document-grain mint, and three seams carry it to every literal scan: `simd.Gate.on` for the required-literal gate, `Emitter.lit_plan` for the hit-jumping sweeps, and `PikeScratch.litPlan` for the span walks.

Two things §7.2 did not anticipate turned up during integration, and both are recorded in `calibrate.zig` rather than shipped as defects. Adopting the sample's favorite unconditionally is a measured CPU tax, not a win, because the shipped table already picks the oracle pair on 80 of 177 code needles and swapping off it also forfeits the single-probe block shape; the fix is an improvement test (`refine`) that prices the incumbent on the same sample. And a purely relative accept margin is a winner's curse, since the argmin of up to 120 noisy density estimates sits several sigma below the truth, so the accept margin is the larger of 12.5% and four standard deviations.

## Measured End to End

Measured end to end on a 200 MB buffer whose alphabet is the statically rare bytes, over three needles whose locally rarest byte the shipped table ranks common, the whole-buffer literal modes cost 6.9–8.0× less CPU (7.8–8.3× for `--json`, 7.0× for `-q`), the bare kernel sweep underneath drops 17.6–17.9× (4.09 million block survivors down to 34–42), the median ratio is 1.002× on rows where the table was already right, and output stays byte-identical across 411 of 411 ripgrep parity cases and 420 in-binary differentials.

A pair-aware policy for the residual ~1.5× to oracle is still ahead. Pincer names the repair: choose the pair jointly, from statistics that can see the correlation, and since no shippable static table can see it (measured, §6), read those statistics off the buffer being searched.

## What Is Ours

A dated adversarial prior-art pass (2026-07-29) leaves less novelty than the measurements might suggest, and the honest split matters.

- **The defect** — the clamp, the tie-break, the `0:1` collapse, and the Certificate blind spot that hid them — is ours.
- **The diagnosis** was published by [Richard Startin](https://richardstartin.github.io/posts/heuristics-for-substring-search) in 2018, in this exact domain, with real-corpus bigram tables. This dossier rediscovered it.
- **Self-calibration is killed as a claim.** Zoekt already picks its two probes from live posting-list lengths, Optimal Seed Solver from the real reference index, and `memchr` ships a trait for exactly this substitution. This kernel should adopt it because it is known-good; what the measurements add is the price (0.2%) that the incumbent's stated "far too expensive" objection assumed without measuring.
- **Narrowly unclaimed** is minimizing `P(X_i = a ∧ X_{i+d} = b)` over concrete byte values at a specific gap, per needle, as the direct objective for a *conjunctive* filter. Buhler–Keich–Sun (2003) and iedera already choose filter positions under an empirical Markov background, so the general move is 23 years old.

Patent coverage in that pass is weak, so nothing here is assertable externally yet. The six specific findings that would kill the remaining claim are in [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md).

## Where the Production Code Lives

Both repairs and the calibrating selector are integrated. The defect lived at [`src/kernel/scan/rarity.zig`](../../src/kernel/scan/rarity.zig) (the clamp) and `src/kernel/scan/simd.zig`'s `indexOfPos` (the tie-break and the two-probe loop). `anchor.zig` now owns the static decision, `calibrate.zig` the per-buffer one, and `simd.zig`'s `Plan`, `planOn`, and `Gate.on` are the seam between them.

The guards ship beside the code: `anchor_test.zig` pins the defect's own invariant, that an all-tied needle must never select adjacent offsets, and `calibrate_test.zig` covers the improvement test, including the uniform-alphabet case where every pair is genuinely identical and the winner's curse would otherwise manufacture a swap. The harnesses that produced the tables in `PROOF.md` were pre-production spikes and are not in this tree; `TESTING.md` records what each established and what a reader can and cannot re-run here.

## Status

This dossier is integrated and measured end to end. The graduation path `TESTING.md` laid out was followed in order: widen the clamp first (a pure dynamic-range fix worth 1.73×/2.06× on its own), then hoist the decision into a value, then calibrate it per document. Selectivity is exact; wall clock and the end-to-end CPU ratios are Apple M4 only, so the hardware-independent re-run on the Anvil box is still owed.

One finding is worth acting on independently of the rest. The Certificate's `literal-rare` rung probes `pgxpool`, whose first two bytes happen to form a rare digraph, which is why a 20× defect sat in a benchmarked hot path undetected. That is a probe-selection blind spot, not a measurement error.
